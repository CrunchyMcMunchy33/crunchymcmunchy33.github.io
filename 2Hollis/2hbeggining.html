The Beginning of 2hollis

A Foundation in Music and Industry

2hollis, born Hollis Parker Frazier-Herndon, was born into a world where music and industry were deeply intertwined. His mother is Kathryn Frazier, a highly influential music publicist and entrepreneur who founded the PR firm Biz 3 and co-founded the record label OWSLA alongside Skrillex. Growing up in Altadena, California, with this background provided him with a unique front-row seat to the mechanics of the music business. Frazier's roster has included artists like Daft Punk, The Weeknd, and J. Cole, meaning her son was exposed from an early age to the narratives and strategies of global superstars.

Early Access and Creative Freedom

Beyond just industry insight, Kathryn Frazier provided her son with the tools to create. At the age of 11, 2hollis was given access to the music software Ableton, a gift that empowered him to begin his own journey of musical experimentation. This early access was crucial, allowing him to develop his production skills years ahead of his peers. His initial forays into music were made under the alias "Drippysoup" starting in 2018, where he developed a sound described as "medieval trap". This period was marked by creative freedom, nurtured by an environment where artistic expression was not just encouraged but was a family business.

The Support of a Single Mother

As a single mother for half of the year due to her ex-husband's touring schedule, Kathryn Frazier was deeply hands-on. She has been vocal about prioritizing her children above all else, stating, "The kids trump everything". This ethos meant that while she was managing the careers of some of music's biggest names, she was also dedicated to being present for her sons. She made a point to drop them off at school, pick them up, and attend their sporting events, refusing to sacrifice these moments for her career. This unwavering support and presence likely created a stable foundation for 2hollis to take risks and pursue his passion with confidence, knowing he had a strong support system behind him.

From Privilege to Pressure

While his connections provided undeniable opportunities, 2hollis's beginnings were also shaped by the unique pressures of his environment. His mother’s success meant he grew up with advantages, but it also placed him in an orbit of high expectations. Kathryn Frazier herself has reflected on her upbringing with a single mother and financial instability, working tirelessly to provide a secure home for her own children. This drive and work ethic were undoubtedly passed down to 2hollis. His emergence from the underground was not just a story of industry connections, but of an artist who, despite his privileged access, put in the work to develop a unique sound and build his own career from the ground up, beginning in his bedroom and evolving into a signed artist with Interscope Records.
