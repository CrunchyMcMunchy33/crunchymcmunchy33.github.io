Growing up

2hollis, born Hollis Chambers, grew up in the suburbs of Atlanta, Georgia, where his early exposure to the internet and underground music scenes shaped much of his creative vision. From a young age, Hollis was drawn to digital culture—particularly the intersection of music, fashion, and visual aesthetics. Like many in his generation, he spent countless hours online, discovering niche communities on platforms like Tumblr and SoundCloud that would heavily influence his later work. This early immersion into alternative and internet-driven culture gave him a DIY ethos and a sharp sense of aesthetic that carried into his adulthood.

In his teenage years, 2hollis started experimenting with music production, graphic design, and clothing. He was self-taught in many ways, learning to make beats and visuals through trial, error, and YouTube tutorials. These skills became foundational as he began building his personal brand—one rooted in anonymity, mystique, and a distinctly digital-era vibe. His work started catching attention not just for its sound, but for the way he fused internet-era nostalgia with clean, forward-thinking design.

Despite not following the traditional path of a musician or artist, 2hollis’s background growing up in an internet-saturated world made him a prime example of the modern multimedia creator. His early life reflects a broader generational shift—where identity, art, and music are all deeply interwoven with online culture.
