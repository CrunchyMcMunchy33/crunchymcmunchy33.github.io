2Hollis's Break Through Moment

The 2024 Tipping Point: "crush," "jeans," and the Album *Boy*

While 2hollis had been building a dedicated following since his 2022 album *White Tiger*, his true breakthrough moment occurred in 2024. This period marked his transition from an underground artist with industry connections to a recognized name with a rapidly growing fanbase. The catalyst was the release of a series of singles, most notably "crush" and "jeans," which resonated deeply with listeners and showcased a more polished and accessible side of his experimental sound. These tracks built significant momentum, culminating in the release of his third studio album, *Boy*, on June 7, 2024. This project served as his definitive statement, blending his unique "medieval trap" origins with the polished sheen of modern electropop and rage, and solidifying his identity in the mainstream music landscape.

From Online Buzz to Live Recognition

The success of *Boy* and its singles was not confined to streaming platforms; it translated directly into real-world opportunities. 2hollis's rising profile earned him a spot as the opening act for Ken Carson's major 2024 tour, *The Chaos Tour*. This was a significant step, placing him in front of large, live audiences and validating his status as a performer capable of holding his own alongside established artists. The positive reception on this tour was so strong that he quickly announced his own first headlining North American tour, *Leg One*, later that same year. This progression from supporting act to headliner in a single year was a clear indicator that his breakthrough was not a fleeting moment but the foundation of a sustainable career.

Cementing Status and Looking Forward

The impact of his 2024 breakthrough carried over into 2025, further cementing his status as an artist on the rise. He was named one of the top 26 artists to watch in 2025 by the publication *Ones to Watch*, placing him alongside other emerging talents like Addison Rae and Mk.gee. This industry recognition was followed by the release of his fourth studio album, *Star*, on April 4, 2025, which marked his debut on the major label Interscope Records. This signing represented the culmination of his breakthrough journey, proving that his unique artistic vision had earned the support and resources of a major industry player. The subsequent *Star Tour* confirmed his global appeal, transforming the promise of his 2024 breakthrough into a tangible, international career.
