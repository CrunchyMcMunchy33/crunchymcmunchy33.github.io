The Evolution Of 2Hollis's Music

The Drippysoup Era: Experimental Foundations

2hollis's musical journey began in 2018 under the alias "Drippysoup." During this formative period, he was already experimenting with a unique sound, with his first songs being described as "medieval trap" by the publication *032c*. This early work laid the groundwork for his genre-blending approach, showcasing a willingness to combine disparate influences into a cohesive, albeit raw, artistic vision. His initial releases, like the EP *Game World*, were self-released and built the foundation for his later, more polished work.

Gaining Traction with *White Tiger* and *2*

The transition from Drippysoup to 2hollis marked a significant evolution. He first gained substantial attention with his 2022 album, *White Tiger*, a self-released project that solidified his identity as an artist capable of crafting immersive, experimental soundscapes. He followed this quickly with his second studio album, *2*, in May 2023. This period saw him refining his production and songwriting, moving further away from the "medieval trap" label and incorporating elements of cloud rap, EDM, and electroclash. A notable milestone during this time was the inclusion of his 2023 song "Poster Boy" in the *EA Sports FC 24* soundtrack, signaling his growing reach beyond underground music circles.

Mainstream Breakthrough with *Boy* and Touring

The year 2024 was 2hollis's breakthrough moment. The release of popular singles like "crush" and "jeans" catapulted him into a new level of recognition, culminating in the release of his third studio album, *Boy*, in June 2024. This album and its associated singles represented the pinnacle of his early career, blending his experimental roots with a more accessible and polished sound that resonated with a wider audience. His rising profile was confirmed by live performances; he opened for Ken Carson's *The Chaos Tour* and soon after announced his first headlining North American tour, *Leg One*, proving his ability to command a stage and connect with fans globally.

Maturation and Major Label Debut with *Star*

In 2025, 2hollis continued his upward trajectory, maturing both as an artist and a public figure. He was named one of the top artists to watch in 2025 by *Ones to Watch*, highlighting his industry impact. He then released his fourth studio album, *Star*, on April 4, 2025, which marked his debut on a major label, Interscope Records. This release signified a new chapter in his career, combining his experimental edge with the resources and reach of a major label. The album was supported by the extensive *Star Tour*, which included dates across Europe, Australia, and Asia, cementing his status as an international artist with a rapidly growing and dedicated fanbase.
